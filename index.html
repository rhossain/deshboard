Questions

Which newspapers are included?+

59 Bangladeshi newspapers and news sites in Bangla and English, among them Prothom Alo, The Daily Star, Jugantor, Kaler Kantho, bdnews24 and Dhaka Tribune, and 31 TV news channels on YouTube. The All newspapers page lists every one.

Is it free?+

Yes. Reading Bartaboard is free, with no ads, and you don't need an account.

How often is it updated?+

Every 15 minutes. When new headlines arrive while you're reading, the page offers them without moving what you're reading.

Does Bartaboard write its own news?+

No. Every headline links to the newspaper that published it, and you read the story on its own site.

Why are some sites read through Google News?+

A few sites block automated reading or publish no feed, so their headlines come from Google News instead. Each site's card says how its headlines were read.

Can I watch TV news here?+

Yes. Videos has the last day's news videos from 31 Bangladeshi TV channels on YouTube, including Jamuna TV, Somoy TV and Channel 24. They play right on the page.
