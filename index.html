Why are some sites read through Google News?+

A few sites block automated reading or publish no feed, so their headlines come from Google News instead. Each site's card says how its headlines were read.

Can I watch TV news here?+

Yes. Videos has the last day's news videos from 31 Bangladeshi TV channels on YouTube, including Jamuna TV, Somoy TV and Channel 24. They play right on the page.
