About Bartaboard

Bartaboard is a Bangladesh news aggregator: the latest headlines from 59 Bangladeshi newspapers and news sites, in Bangla and English, and the latest videos from 31 TV news channels, on one board. It is free, and you don't need an account to read it.

How it works

  • Every 15 minutes Bartaboard reads each site's own news feed, its sitemap for search engines or its front page, and for the few that can't be read directly, Google News. It keeps the headline, the link and the time, and sorts each story into a section: national, politics, sports and so on.
  • Headlines link straight to the newspaper that published them. Bartaboard doesn't copy articles; you read them on the publisher's own site.
  • Top stories groups the same event reported by several outlets, by matching their headlines. The more outlets cover it, the higher it ranks.
  • Videos shows the last day's news videos from the TV channels' YouTube channels, newest first. They play here or on YouTube.
  • The idea of reading every Bangladeshi newspaper from one page comes from 24 Live Newspaper (24livenewspaper.com); Bartaboard builds on it with live headlines, top stories and TV news videos.

Free, with ads and paid plans to come

Reading Bartaboard is free, and it will stay free.

  • Today there are no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies. Your saved stories, pinned sources and settings stay in your own browser (see the privacy policy).
  • Ads. To pay for running Bartaboard, we plan to show ads. They will be marked as ads and kept apart from the headlines, and before the first one appears the privacy policy will say who shows them and what they collect.
  • Paid plans. We also plan optional paid plans for extras such as keyword alerts. Headlines, Videos, saving and pinning stay free, with no account needed. A plan's price, what it includes and how refunds work are shown before you pay (see the terms).

The sources

All newspapers and channels lists every outlet Bartaboard reads, with links to its own site. Source health shows how each one was read on the last update and why any is missing.

Add a newspaper or channel

Missing a Bangladeshi news site or TV channel, or publish one and want something changed? Write to support@bartaboard.com with its address. Bartaboard is independent and not affiliated with any of the outlets it lists.
