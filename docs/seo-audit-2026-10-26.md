on the live HTML today, or in the source. Each item carries its status as the work goes: ✅ done (with the commit), ⬜ open, 🟡 partly.

## P0: what decides whether the site ranks at all

### 1. Half the outlet pages are not in Google's index, and nothing links to them

🟡 **Code done (27 Oct):** every source card on the board and the section pages, in both languages, now links
to its outlet page twice: the logo/name in the header (desktop; on phones the header is the fold's tap target)
and a "More from Prothom Alo →" line at the card's foot, with the outlet's own site beside it. 64 outlet links
on each of the 28 board pages, where there were none. **Still to do by hand:** export the Pages report, request
indexing for the 35 English outlet pages; the extra own text per outlet page is open.

Search Console on 12 October: 92 indexed, **49 "crawled – currently not indexed"**, of which 45 were outlet
pages (`/sources/<id>/`, mostly English pages of Bangla outlets). Profiles were added on 15 October; there has
been no Pages report since, so the current state is unknown.

Checked today on `/sources/ittefaq/` vs `/bn/sources/ittefaq/`: the pair still shares 53 % of its words
(same 40 headlines, same counts); the English page has about 190 English words of its own. Enough to differ,
possibly not enough for Google to keep both.

The bigger cause: **no page on the site links to an outlet page except `/sources/`.** The board and the 12
section pages (305 links each) link every source card's name to the *publisher's* site, and nowhere to
`/sources/<id>/`. The 81 outlet pages hang off one hub, two clicks from the home page, with one internal link
each. Google treats that as low-value.

Fix:
- Export the Pages report from Search Console now (before any change), and again two weeks after.
- Link each source card to its outlet page from the board and every section page, in both languages: the
  card header (logo/name) → `/sources/<id>/`, with the publisher link kept as the "Website ↗" action; or a
  "More from Prothom Alo →" line under the card's last headline. That gives every outlet page 28 internal
  links from the site's strongest pages.
- Link the outlet page from the headline actions menu ("More from this outlet") and from Top stories'
  outlet names.
- Give the English page of a Bangla outlet something the Bangla page lacks (and vice-versa): e.g. an
  English line on each of its sections ("Ittefaq's sports desk covers cricket…"), the outlet's English
  edition link where one exists (`bdnews24-en`), and "also read through Google News" notes. 80–120 more own
  words per page is the target, not more headlines.
- Request indexing (URL inspection) for the 35 English outlet pages, 10 a day, plus `/news/crime/`,
  `/news/opinion/`, `/videos/`, `/about/`, `/sources/`, which were never requested.

## P1: technical SEO defects

### 4. Every Bangla page says `<html lang="en">`

✅ **Done (27 Oct):** `scripts/after-build.ts`, run by `npm run build` after `next build`, writes `lang="bn"` into
the 98 exported Bangla pages; the layout's script now only serves the 404/share page.

All 98 `/bn/…` pages ship `lang="en"`; a 1-line inline script flips it to `bn` after the HTML is parsed
(`LANG_SCRIPT` in `layout.tsx`, because one root layout serves both languages for the static 404). Google
ignores `lang`, but Bing, Yandex, the AI assistants' crawlers, translation prompts in browsers and screen
readers all read the attribute, and hreflang is more trusted when it agrees with it.

Fix (cheapest): a post-build step in `deploy.yml` after `next build` that rewrites `<html lang="en"` →
`<html lang="bn"` in `out/bn/**/index.html` (one `sed`/`tsx` script, ~10 lines); keep `LANG_SCRIPT` for the
404/share page. Cleaner: two root layouts via route groups (`app/(en)/layout.tsx`, `app/(bn)/bn/layout.tsx`)
with the 404 page under the English group.

### 5. Bangla pages don't announce their RSS feeds

✅ **Done (27 Oct):** `/bn/`, `/bn/news/<section>/` and `/bn/videos/` link the shared feeds with Bangla titles.

`/`, `/news/<section>/`, `/videos/` carry `<link rel="alternate" type="application/rss+xml">`; `/bn/`,
`/bn/news/…`, `/bn/videos/` don't (`alternates.types` missing from the bn pages' metadata). Trivial.

### 6. The 404 page is titled like the home page

✅ **Done (27 Oct):** "Page not found | Bartaboard" with its own description; shared `/s/…` pages still get the headline's title from the Function.

`/nope/` returns 404 with `<title>Bangladesh News Today, Bangla & English | Bartaboard</title>` and the home
description. Harmless for ranking (noindex) but it is what appears in browser tabs and in Search Console's
"Not found" report, and confuses "soft 404" checks. Fix: `title: "Page not found"` in `not-found.tsx`'s
metadata (the share-page variant keeps rewriting it in the Function).

### 7. The site's own words sit at the bottom of a 730 KB page, and the H1 is 13 px grey

✅ **Done (27 Oct):** the H1 is 15–16 px semibold in the text colour; one sentence about the page (the
section's own, or the board's/videos') sits under it, above the search box, in the page's language; the foot
keeps the rest. The `sr-only` H2s are left as they were.

On the board the intro paragraphs (the only text about Bartaboard on the page) start at character
600,000 of 744,000 of the body; the H1 renders at 13 px in the muted colour, visually a subtitle. Google
weights content by prominence; the current layout says "the headlines are the page, our text is a footnote".

Fix: one visible sentence from the intro under the H1 (above the search box), in body size; the H1 at
16 px/normal weight (it needn't be big, just not muted); the rest of the intro can stay at the foot. Same on
section pages, with the section's sentence. The `sr-only` "Headlines by newspaper" and "Top stories" H2s are
acceptable; a visible "By newspaper" label would be better than hidden.

### 8. One share image for all 196 pages, and it is in English on the Bangla pages

🟡 **Partly (27 Oct):** the image's three fonts are in the repository now (`src/app/fonts/og/`) instead of being
fetched from jsdelivr at build time, which failed the build wherever that CDN is unreachable (its "falls back"
comment was wrong: the renderer needs at least one font). Per-page images and `fb:app_id` are still open.

`/opengraph-image` (the site card) is the `og:image` of every page. Facebook is the site's main channel; a
section page shared there looks identical to the home page, and `/bn/…` shares show English text. Also no
`fb:app_id` meta, so Facebook's insights can't attribute the domain's shares to the app (`FB_APP_ID` is
public and in `wrangler.toml`).

Fix: `opengraph-image.tsx` in `news/[category]/`, `sources/[id]/`, `videos/` and their `bn/` twins
(Next generates them at build; static export supports it), with the section name / outlet logo and the
Bangla title on Bangla pages. Add `other: { "fb:app_id": FB_APP_ID }` to the root metadata.

### 9. Structured-data gaps (small, cheap)

🟡 **Mostly done (27 Oct):** `datePublished` on every `CollectionPage`/`AboutPage`/`WebSite` (the date each kind of
page went live, `FIRST_PUBLISHED` in `site.ts`); `Organization` has `foundingDate`, `email`, a `contactPoint`
and the Bangla `alternateName`. **Open, needs a decision:** a named maker ("Who makes it" on the About page +
`founder` in the schema) — not added without the owner saying what name to publish.

- `CollectionPage` has `dateModified` but no `datePublished`; add the page's first-publish date.
- `Organization` has no `foundingDate`, `email`/`contactPoint`, or `founder` → add; the About page says
  "independent" but never says who makes it. A "Who makes it" line with a name and a `Person` in the
  Organization schema is the E-E-A-T signal an aggregator can give.
- `WebSite.inLanguage` and `CollectionPage.inLanguage` use `["en","bn"]` on some pages and `"en"`/`"bn"` on
  others; fine, but be consistent (array where both languages appear).
- Keep `FAQPage` (Google no longer shows it for sites like this; harmless, useful for AI answers).

### 10. Logo caching and sizes

🟡 **Partly (27 Oct):** `/logos/*` and `/brand/*` are cached a week with a day of stale-while-revalidate (Pages and Apache alike). Logos stay at 3× their drawn size: the 2–7 KB a 2× cut would save is lazy-loaded anyway, and 3× screens are common on phones here.

`/logos/*` and `/brand/*` are cached 24 h (Lighthouse flags it on every page). Logos change at most weekly
and keep their address, so they can't go immutable, but `max-age=604800, stale-while-revalidate=86400` is
safe; better, fingerprint them at fetch time (`prothomalo.<hash>.webp`) and go `immutable` like `_next/`.
`dailystar-bn.webp` is 10.6 KB for a ~120 px slot (7 KB wasted); resize logos at fetch to 2× their display
width. `/brand/*` (your own files) can be immutable now.

## P2: performance (the board pages)

Lab is 89–93 on the board pages; the goal is field LCP < 2.5 s and INP < 200 ms on real Bangladeshi
phones, which means fewer bytes on the critical path and less main-thread work after it.

### 11. 290 KB of JavaScript on every page, most of it not needed at first paint

🟡 **Partly (27 Oct), with a correction.** A source-map breakdown of the board's 15 chunks (900 KB raw) shows
438 KB is React and Next themselves (react-dom alone 195 KB), ~350 KB the site's code; the dialogs and panels
(Tour, share, pins, shortcuts, return prompt) add up to ~25 KB raw, ~7 KB compressed, so lazy-loading them is
not worth the refactor and is dropped. The "legacy JavaScript" Lighthouse flags is Next's own 1 KB polyfill
module, not a browserslist problem. **Done:** the headline-keyword table (24 KB raw) that sorts headlines into
sections — fetch-time work — shipped to every browser because it shared a module with the section list;
it is `src/lib/categorize.ts` now and leaves the page bundle (279 KB compressed where it was 291). Next cuts:
item 15 (the sources config and the seed), and `strings.ts` carrying both languages to every page (16 KB raw).

Two app chunks (72 KB + 44 KB Brotli) carry 49 KB that is never run on load; `NewsBoard.tsx` (~2,000 lines)
is one client component that ships the Tour, the share dialog, keyboard-shortcuts dialog, PlanPanel,
AccountPanel, PinPicker, the Filters sheet and the search machinery up front. Only the Videos view is
lazy-loaded. Lighthouse also finds 13 KB of polyfills (`Array.prototype.at` etc.) because the TypeScript
target is ES2017.

Fix: `lazy()`/`next/dynamic` for every dialog, sheet and panel (same pattern as `LazyVideoBoard`), so the
first bundle is the board and the card list; set `browserslist` to modern browsers (`last 2 Chrome/Safari/
Firefox versions, Android >= 10`) so Next drops the polyfills. Target: < 200 KB on the board, and measure
by chunk after each step.

### 12. Three analytics scripts run on every load, right after hydration

✅ **Done (27 Oct), with one change of plan:** all three now start only once the page has loaded and the browser
is idle (`src/lib/settled.ts`; at most 4 s after load), so none of them competes with hydration or the reader's
first taps. The Cloudflare beacon is **kept**, not dropped: it is the smallest of the three and the only one that
reports real readers' Core Web Vitals (item 19), and the privacy page describes it. Checked in a headless phone:
GA, the Pixel and the beacon all load after the page settles; the token still reaches the page (it is decided by
`SITE_URL` at build time, so the layout passes it in).

Cloudflare Web Analytics (`beacon.min.js`), Google Analytics (`gtag/js`) and the Meta Pixel (`fbevents.js`)
all start in `useEffect` outside the EU. They are after first paint, so they don't move LCP, but on a
low-end Android they add well over 100 KB and hundreds of ms of main-thread time exactly when the reader
starts tapping (INP). GA and Cloudflare also measure the same thing.

Fix: drop the Cloudflare beacon (GA now covers visits; update the privacy page, which names all three);
start GA and the Pixel from `requestIdleCallback` (fallback: `setTimeout` 3 s) or on first interaction, and
never before the head file has loaded. Keep the Pixel's `PageView` semantics intact (it fires late, not
less).

### 13. 157 KB of preloaded fonts, with weights the site never uses

✅ **Done (27 Oct), pulled forward:** the fonts are self-hosted (`src/app/fonts/`, see its README), cut to
weights 400–700 with fontTools; the preloaded pair is 112 KB (Inter 36 KB, Noto Sans Bengali 76 KB) instead of
157 KB, Noto Sans Bengali no longer carries Latin faces Inter answers for anyway, and a build fetches nothing
from Google (which this machine's network can't reach, so builds here failed until this).

Noto Sans Bengali variable (108 KB, wght 100–900) and Inter variable (49 KB, wght 100–900) are preloaded;
the site uses 400/500/600/700 only (270 `font-semibold`, 28 `font-medium`, 4 `font-bold`). The Bangla font
is on the LCP path (the LCP element is Bangla text). The 12 October test showed removing the preload hurts;
shrinking the files does not.

Fix: instance both variable fonts to `wght 400:700` with fonttools (`varLib.instancer -w wght=400:700`),
and serve them with `next/font/local` (same `display: swap`, same `unicode-range` split). Expected 30–40 %
smaller. Option B, bigger win: drop Inter for the system UI stack (`system-ui, -apple-system, Segoe UI,
Roboto`) and keep only Noto Sans Bengali; a design call.

### 14. 4,700–4,900 DOM elements on board pages (Lighthouse's line is 1,500)

🟡 **Partly (27 Oct):** one shared popover menu for every headline (`ItemMenuHost` in `ui.tsx`; each ⋮ is now
just its button) and the desktop Filters panel renders its controls, the 65-option source list included, only
while open. About 300 elements fewer on the board (4,800 in a headless phone, 5,100 before), checked by
opening, moving, saving from, closing and re-toggling the menu. What is left is the prerendered headlines
themselves (207 × ~7 elements) and the cards: fewer would mean less for crawlers, so they stay.

Style/layout is 311 ms of main-thread time per load. Where they come from: 207 prerendered headlines ×
~8 elements each, **208 prerendered popover menus (one per headline)**, 5 `<dialog>`s, a 65-option source
`<select>`, and the desktop section nav plus the mobile category tabs both in the HTML.

Fix: one shared popover menu for all headlines (positioned at the button that opened it) instead of 208;
render dialogs only when open (they already hydrate client-side); build the source `<select>` options on
focus; trim the per-headline markup (the `<time>` and title can be one link with two spans, the ⋮ button
one element). Target: < 2,500 without prerendering fewer headlines.

### 15. The HTML repeats every prerendered headline in the React payload

✅ **Done (27 Oct):** the seed reaches the board packed (`seedForPage` in `feed.ts`, unpacked once in
`NewsBoardFromUrl`) and without the fetch's record of where it read each source; the fetch addresses and
article patterns moved out of `sources.ts` into `source-feeds.ts`, which only the fetch loads. The board's
React payload is 94 KB raw where it was 131 (HTML 71 → 68 KB compressed), and the scraper's configuration is
no longer in every page.

Of the board's 730 KB raw HTML, 131 KB is `self.__next_f` data: the `seed` prop serialised as full
`NewsItem` objects (`sourceId`, `sourceName`, `lang` and ISO times repeated on each of 266 headlines), on
top of the rendered cards, plus the whole `SOURCES` fetch configuration as a prop (every outlet's feed URL,
`method`, `notes`, `fetchedUrl`, `durationMs`), which the browser never needs and which documents the
scraper in public. Compressed it is ~15–20 KB of the 55 KB, and all of it is parsed before hydration.

Fix: pass the seed to the client component **packed** (the `PackedSeed` arrays `packed.ts` already
defines) and unpack in the browser; pass only the display fields of each source (id, name, Bangla name,
language, kind, homepage), not the fetch config, and strip `fetchedUrl`/`durationMs`/`method` from seed
results. ~50 % smaller payload, no change to what crawlers see.

### 16. Early Hints for the CSS and the two fonts

The CSS (render-blocking) and the two preloaded fonts are discovered only after the 55 KB HTML arrives.
Cloudflare Pages sends `103 Early Hints` from `Link:` headers in `_headers`, but the chunk names are hashed
per build. Fix: a post-build step that writes the current CSS/font `Link: </…>; rel=preload; as=style`
lines into `out/_headers` for `/`, `/bn/`, `/news/*`, `/bn/news/*`; turn Early Hints on in Cloudflare
(Speed → Optimization). Worth 100–200 ms of simulated LCP on mobile.

### 17. Speculation Rules for section and outlet links

✅ **Done (27 Oct):** a `speculationrules` script in the layout prefetches the board, section, outlet, videos and
about pages (both languages) as the pointer settles on their link, `eagerness: moderate`; filtered views
(`?view=…`) are excluded by matching on an empty `search`. Prefetch only, not prerender: a prerendered board
would count a visit and report a page view nobody saw. Checked in Chromium: hovering a section tab fetches
the page with `Sec-Purpose: prefetch`; the saved view is not fetched.

Pages are plain `<a>`s (no Next `<Link>` prefetch in static export). A
`<script type="speculationrules">` with `prerender` on hover/viewport for `/news/*`, `/bn/news/*`,
`/sources/*` makes those navigations instant in Chrome/Edge (the majority on Android), and prerendered
navigations report near-zero LCP in CrUX. Ten lines in the root layout; no downside on other browsers.

### 18. Above-the-fold logos are lazy-loaded

✅ **Done (27 Oct):** the first three cards' logos load with the page (`eager` on SourceCard); the rest stay lazy.

All 64 card logos carry `loading="lazy"`, including the first two or three cards' (in view on every
screen). Not the LCP, but a visible delay. `loading="eager"` for the first two cards, `fetchpriority="high"`
for the first.

### 19. Confirm the field numbers

Search Console → Core Web Vitals (needs ~28 days of Chrome users) and, if empty, `web-vitals` attribution
sent to GA as events (LCP, INP, CLS with the element/interaction target). Without this, 11–18 are being
tuned blind.

## Not worth doing

- `<meta name="keywords">`: still present, still ignored. Drop it in passing; no effect either way.
- Sitemap `changefreq`/`priority`: ignored by Google; leave.
- Google News / Discover / per-headline pages: an aggregator doesn't qualify, and per-headline pages risk a
  manual action. `/s/…` stays noindex.
- Sitelinks search box (`SearchAction`): Google retired it in 2024.
- `Disallow: /data/` in robots: it would stop Googlebot's renderer fetching `head/*.json` and change what it
  sees; `X-Robots-Tag: noindex` on `/data/*` is the right tool and is in place.
- Removing `target="_blank"` on outbound links or adding `nofollow`: the links are editorial; `noopener` is
  set; leave.
